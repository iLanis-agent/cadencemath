Cadence Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts whole days from the dates and cycles you type. It does not say how often anything should be done, or whether a schedule is enough for it.

The next truth

The date of the nth time something happens every N days. The start date is occurrence 1.

The count truth

How many times it happens from the start through an end date, both included.

The lap truth

Two cycles that start on the same day, and the day they line up again.
